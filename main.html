04 / Before we begin

Useful answers,
without the pitch.

Can you build from an existing design?

Yes. Most projects begin with an approved design from a client, designer, or agency. I preserve its typography, spacing, hierarchy, and interaction logic while making it work responsively.

Which platforms do you work with?

Primarily Webflow, Weblium, and Squarespace, with custom HTML, CSS, and JavaScript where the platform needs more flexibility.

Is SEO part of development?

Technical and on-page foundations are part of the build: semantic structure, headings, metadata, media handling, internal links, indexing controls, and performance.

Can the client update the site?

Yes. CMS collections, reusable components, and editable sections are organized around the team’s real publishing workflow, with guidance included at handoff.
