MFG Matters

A responsive, client-editable platform connecting manufacturing consulting, technical learning, travel, and community.

Webflow developer

Overview

A multi-part business developed as one manageable website.

MFG Matters combines consulting, learning, technical travel, and merchandise. I converted the provided direction into a modular Webflow build that separates each offer clearly while giving the client a consistent structure for updates.

The development challenge

Give every business line room to grow.

What the build needed to do

Support different content types and audiences while keeping the code-free editing experience predictable for the team.

  • Build distinct but connected sections
  • Make workshops easy to maintain
  • Preserve responsive consistency
  • Create search-friendly page structure

Development approach

Modular for the developer. Simple for the client.

Reusable layout patterns and CMS structures let the business add offers and learning content without losing visual consistency or touching the underlying build.

01

Shared patterns keep Consulting, Learning, and Travel connected while supporting different content needs.

02

03

Distinct page topics, semantic hierarchy, and internal pathways help each offer remain discoverable.
