Overview

A healthcare experience built for trust and ongoing publishing.

QHSLab serves physicians, patients, and investors with a growing library of clinical and educational content. My role was to translate the visual direction into Squarespace, extend the platform where needed, and keep content publishing accessible to the internal team.

The development challenge

Balance custom presentation with simple editing.

What the build needed to do

Support audience-specific journeys, a large learning hub, and conversion pages without making routine updates dependent on a developer.

  • Extend Squarespace responsively
  • Organize educational content
  • Strengthen technical SEO basics
  • Keep publishing client-friendly

Development approach

Customized where it matters. Editable where it counts.

Squarespace’s content tools handle the ongoing publishing workflow, while targeted styling and code refinements preserve the approved design and improve responsive behavior.
